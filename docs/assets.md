## Architecture diagram

`docs/architecture.svg` · 1240 × 780 · hand-authored, no tooling. Dark `#0D1117` ground.

Shows, left to right: Browser → Caddy :80 → FastAPI :8080 (guard: 12/h per IP, one at a time) →
the orchestrator's five steps (plan, build, import, resolve, verdict) → the sandbox container
with its flags (`--read-only`, `--user 10001`, `--cap-drop ALL`, `--pids-limit 64`,
`--memory 512m`, `--tmpfs /tmp:noexec`) → the deny proxy → evidence (`egress.jsonl`,
`fsdiff.json`, `exec.json`, plus self-reported `probe.json` / `import_probe.json`) → verdict.

Two annotations on the diagram: "resolve gets network — alone" (the one container that does) and
the footer lines stating that the model may raise but not lower the risk, and that dependency
wheels are hashed and listed, never installed.
