## Architecture diagram

`docs/architecture.svg` · 1240 × 780 · hand-authored, no tooling. Dark `#0D1117` ground.

Shows, left to right: Browser → Caddy :80 → FastAPI :8080 (guard: 12/h per IP, one at a time) →
the orchestrator's five steps (plan, build, import, resolve, verdict) → the sandbox container
with its flags (`--read-only`, `--user 10001`, `--cap-drop ALL`, `--pids-limit 64`,
`--memory 512m`, `--tmpfs /tmp:noexec`) → the deny proxy → evidence (`egress.jsonl`,
`fsdiff.json`, `exec.json`, plus self-reported `probe.json` / `import_probe.json`) → verdict.

Two annotations on the diagram: "resolve gets network — alone" (the one container that does) and
the footer lines stating that the model may raise but not lower the risk, and that dependency
wheels are hashed and listed, never installed.

## Recording

`recordings/demo.mp4` (gitignored) · 2:34 · 1440×732 · dark · **silent**.
Produced by `tools/record-demo.sh`, which reads the run ids from the API, drives Safari in one
tab, records the screen and crops the browser chrome off. The holds follow `docs/transcript.md`
beat for beat, so the narration fits without re-timing.

Crop note, learned the hard way: `screencapture -v` ignores `-R` and records the whole screen.
The page starts at y≈200 px in the 2880-wide capture, and libx264 refuses an odd height.

Earlier experiments that are no longer files of record: a `probe.png` crop test (y=190/194)
that showed the page edge 6 px off, and the light-theme check measured 240–250 across sidebar,
topbar and content — i.e. the light palette is complete, not half-applied.

## Superseded UI generations (kept as facts, not files)

Three layouts existed before the current one. Their screenshots are no longer referenced; what
they showed, in case a number is needed later:

- **Version 1** — single column, hero card with risk/model/duration, four stat cards under it,
  then Plan / Verdict / Findings / Evidence / Trace as separate panels, task form always open.
  Numbers on screen at the time: 3 runs, 1 blocked, median 14 s.
- **Version 2** — sidebar with a run rail, tabs (Summary / Dependencies / Evidence), form in a
  dialog. Numbers: 5 runs, 1 blocked, median 13.4 s.
- **Version 3 (current)** — the same, plus the top bar with workspace, model chip and account.

The `probe.png` and `probe_190/194.png` files were crop-offset tests, not product states: they
showed the page edge 6 px off, which is why the recording script crops at y=198.
